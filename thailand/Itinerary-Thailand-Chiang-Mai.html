Good To Know

VisaVisa-free for Malaysians — the 60-day exemption still applies on 7 Sept (30 days from 15 Sept, irrelevant for 5 nights).
MoneyThai baht. Cards fine in the city; cash for markets, temples, park entry.
Getting aroundGrab and Bolt everywhere; songthaews ~30–40 THB a hop. The two mountain days come with a driver.
WeatherGreen season: ~33°C, humid, ~18 rainy days in September. Showers come late afternoon or overnight — mornings are the clear window.
Temple dressShoulders and knees covered at every temple, both of you. A light scarf handles it.
Why hereWaterfalls at full flow, terraces at their greenest, and the quietest, cheapest month of the year.
