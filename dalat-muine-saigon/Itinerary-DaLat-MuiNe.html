Good To Know

Halal, honestlyDa Lat has two halal kitchens, both in the city. Nothing on the tourist routes is halal — plan meals around the city, treat cafés as drinks-only.
VisaVisa-free for Malaysians, 30 days. Carry hotel bookings and onward tickets.
Three climatesDa Lat 17–24°C, cold before dawn; Mui Ne 25–31°C; Saigon 30°C+ and humid.
MoneyVietnamese dong. Cash for entry tickets (100–120k a head), dune jeeps and markets.
Getting aroundGrab in Da Lat and Saigon; a booked car for the out-of-town days. Mui Ne is one strip.
Two early starts04.15 on Day 2 and 05.00 on Day 4, both for light you cannot get later. Afternoons are deliberately empty.

Halal food — the whole list

Da Lat has two halal kitchens. Everything else on your list is a drinks-only stop.
PlaceWhereWhat to know
Fatimah Restaurant📍 Open in Google Maps18–24 Nguyễn Du, Ward 9, Da LatHalal Vietnamese, with a prayer space. The better of the two for a sit-down dinner. Day 1 dinner is built around it.

Your hotel breakfast📍 Open in Google MapsDa Lat and Mui NeAsk for eggs, bread, fruit, noodles without the pork broth. The simplest safe meal of each day, and it's already paid for.
How to eat well on this trip
  • None of the cafés on your list are halal kitchens — Thác Mây, Haiyih, Miền Du Mục, Lạc Hư Cổ Trấn, Reply 1994, Suối Bình Yên and the rest. Coffee, tea and packaged drinks are fine; treat their food menus as off the list.
  • Day 2 is the hard one: you're out east from 04.15 with no halal food anywhere near Cầu Đất. Take breakfast and snacks from the city and keep dinner at Halal Food Da Lat before it closes at 20:30.
  • Night market: fruit, roasted chestnuts, corn and hot soy milk are fine. The grilled-meat stalls are not.

Where to stay, and why

One central Da Lat base covers every cluster; one Ham Tien resort covers Mui Ne
HotelRough priceWhy this one, and how to book
DA LAT — stay by Xuan Huong Lake—Every cluster on your list radiates out from the centre: Cầu Đất east, Tà Nung west, Lạc Dương north, Datanla south. A lakeside hotel is equidistant from all of them, and puts the lake, the night market, L'angfarm and both halal restaurants within walking distance. Golden Sun Hotel Dalat$$Three minutes' walk from the lake, parking on site, straightforward and central. The efficient middle option. Find it & book
Peach Valley Hotel Dalat$Ten minutes from the lake, terrace, own parking — the value pick if you'd rather spend on the drivers. Find it & book
MUI NE — stay on the Ham Tien strip—Everything in Mui Ne is on one road: Fairy Stream, the fishing village and Mughal House are all minutes away, and the dune jeeps collect from the hotel. Browse the strip: booking.com — all Mui Ne hotels
Anantara Mui Ne Resort$$$The established high-end choice on Ham Tien, beachfront with big pools. Worth it when the plan is a deliberately empty afternoon. Find it & book
Ham Tien beachfront, 4-star$$The strip is full of good four-stars with private beach and two pools. Filter for beachfront and 8.0+ and take whatever is cheapest for your dates — they are much of a muchness. Browse the strip
Booking notes
  • Prices move a lot with the date; I have not checked live rates, so treat $ / $$ / $$$ as relative only.
  • In Da Lat, ask for a room away from the street — the centre is noisy until late and you are getting up at 03.45.
  • In Mui Ne, ask the resort to arrange the dune jeep and the Saigon car when you check in. It is cheaper than booking either from Malaysia.
  • No hotel on this trip is halal-certified. Breakfast is easy to navigate; dinner comes from the food page.
